Integrated Pest Management

Integrated Pest Management (IPM) is a practical approach to crop protection that combines monitoring, prevention, biological methods, cultural practices, and carefully selected treatments to keep pest populations under control.

Instead of relying on a single control method, IPM focuses on understanding pest behaviour, crop conditions, and economic thresholds to support healthier crops and more responsible pest management decisions.

IPM Approach: Monitor pest activity first, prevent outbreaks where possible, and use the least disruptive control method when intervention becomes necessary.

Key Pest Management Practices

Natural Pest Control

Support natural pest regulation by protecting beneficial insects, predators, parasitoids, and other biological organisms that help maintain crop health.

Eco-Friendly Solutions

Learn about responsible botanical and organic approaches that can help manage common crop pests while supporting healthier and more sustainable farming systems.

Crop Monitoring

Regular field scouting, visual inspection, and suitable monitoring tools help detect pest activity early and support timely crop protection decisions.

Preventive Farming Practices

Apply crop rotation, field sanitation, proper spacing, resistant varieties, intercropping, and timely cultivation to reduce pest pressure before serious damage occurs.
